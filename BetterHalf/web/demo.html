Better Half · two-phone test

Both phones are the real app and sync with each other through this browser. Everything you do on one shows up on the other.
How to test: sign in on both phones (choose Man on the left and Woman on the right). On the left, copy the 6-digit code. On the right, choose I have a code and enter it. After that, try reactions on Home, chat, calls, games, the calendar and routines. Turn a phone off to see actions queue up and arrive when it comes back on.
